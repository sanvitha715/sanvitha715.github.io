Predicting Song Popularity from Spotify Audio Features

STAT 3400 — Applied Regression · R · 2026

Ran EDA, feature engineering, and correlation analysis on 18,835 Spotify tracks, then built and validated a multiple linear regression via forward stepwise selection and 5-fold cross-validation — using RMSE, adjusted R², ANOVA, and residual diagnostics to isolate the strongest predictors of popularity.

Obesity, Food Access & Socioeconomic Conditions Across U.S. Counties

INFO 2201 — Programming for Information Science 2 · Python · 2025

Engineered an integrated county-level dataset by cleaning, transforming, and merging CDC, USDA, and U.S. Census (ACS) sources on FIPS identifiers, then showed through EDA and correlation analysis that obesity prevalence reflects multiple interacting factors rather than any single dominant predictor.

Loggerhead Sea Turtle Population Dynamics

APPM 3310 — Matrix Methods & Applications · Python · 2026

Built a 7×7 stage-structured matrix model and used eigenvalue analysis and diagonalization to compute a dominant eigenvalue of λ ≈ 0.945, isolating late-juvenile and sub-adult survival as the highest-leverage drivers of population growth.

About

I'm Sanvitha — a Statistics and Data Science major at the University of Colorado Boulder, minoring in Information Science and graduating in May 2027. I'm drawn to the questions behind the data: I like taking a real-world problem, digging into the messy parts, and building a model that actually says something useful.

My experience runs across data analytics and generative AI — from tracking down revenue-reporting discrepancies hidden in hundreds of millions of records to building retrieval-augmented systems in the cloud — and I move comfortably between Python and R. I also do human–computer interaction research at CU's ACME Lab, where I run participatory design studies and think a lot about making technology work for everyone, not just the average user.

Outside of coursework and research, I build small arcade games for fun, which is where I get to play with design, feel, and the more creative side of problem-solving. Whatever I'm working on, I like doing it carefully — and making the result something people can actually use.
